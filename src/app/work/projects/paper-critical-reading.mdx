## What it does

Paper Critical Reading pushes past a surface-level summary. Instead of restating a paper's
abstract, it works through a chain of questions: why the problem exists, why the authors chose
this design, what insight the contribution rests on, what the experiments actually support, and
where the limitations are.

## How it works

Every claim can be checked against the source. Ask for a citation and it retrieves the original
text from the PDF, verifies it, and links out to the exact highlighted passage, down to the line —
so explanations stay grounded in evidence rather than plausible-sounding paraphrase. Papers can be
imported from an arXiv link, a raw PDF, or a DOI.

The same reading pipeline is exposed through a chat interface (Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex),
a local web reader with real PDF page images, CLI tools, and an MCP server. Built in Python 3.10+,
with papers cached locally.
